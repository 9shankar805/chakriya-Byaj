## LONG DESCRIPTION (English)

# Chakriya Byaj - Compound Interest Calculator

Calculate compound interest and simple interest easily with this beautiful, user-friendly app! Perfect for financial calculations, especially in the Nepali context.

## KEY FEATURES

✅ **Compound Interest Calculator** - Calculate compound interest with dates (Nepali-style)
✅ **Simple Interest Calculator** - Quick simple interest calculations
✅ **History of Records** - Save and view all your calculation history
✅ **Nepali & English Languages** - Switch between Nepali and English anytime
✅ **Dark & Light Themes** - Choose your preferred theme (dark, light, or system)
✅ **Beautiful UI** - Clean, modern design with smooth animations
✅ **Local Storage Only** - All data stays on your device, 100% privacy

## HOW TO USE

### Compound Interest
1. Enter the date when the amount was taken (year, month, day)
2. Enter the date when the amount will be returned
3. Enter the principal amount (mul dhan)
4. Enter the monthly interest rate (%)
5. Press Calculate!

### Simple Interest
1. Enter principal amount
2. Enter monthly interest rate (%)
3. Enter time period (months or years)
4. Press Calculate!

Save your calculations with borrower names and optional notes for future reference!
